About Us

VR Designs India Pvt Ltd provides BIM, civil, structural, architectural and survey-related design services. We focus on practical, accurate and coordinated solutions for construction and infrastructure projects.

Our Comprehensive Services

Advanced Surveying & Geospatial

High-accuracy ground surveying and layout solutions utilizing state-of-the-art equipment:

  • Layout Surveys & Plot Demarcation
  • Column & Footing Marking
  • Road Leveling & Earthwork Profiling
  • Total Station Precision Surveys
  • DGPS (Differential GPS) Satellite Mapping

BIM Modeling (Revit)

Comprehensive Building Information Modeling solutions to streamline construction workflow:

  • 3D Parametric Architectural & Structural Modeling
  • Multi-discipline Clash Detection & Coordination
  • Scan-to-BIM & Point Cloud Integration
  • Detailed Shop Drawings & Material Takeoffs

Architectural & Structural Design

Innovative and safe structural planning tailored for residential, commercial, and industrial projects:

  • Concept to Detailed Architectural Planning
  • RCC & Steel Structural Design Calculations
  • Structural Dynamics & Safety Compliance
  • Estimation, Costing & Execution Supervision
